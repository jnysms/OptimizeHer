What You'll Discover

Most reference ranges were built for men. Women's bodies were left out of research entirely.

Did You Know?

  • Women were excluded from most clinical trials in the US until 1993 — meaning decades of drug approvals and reference ranges were built almost entirely on male data. NIH Office of Research on Women's Health
  • As of 2011, over 70% of US and Canadian medical schools reported having no formal gender-specific curriculum — meaning most doctors were never taught how women's bodies differ. PMC / Journal of Medical Education
  • Most standard lab reference ranges are based on male physiology and are still routinely applied to women today. Association of American Medical Colleges

The Science Behind It

This isn't a wellness trend. It's traceable research.

Every optimal range in OptimizeHer is sourced directly from internationally recognized health authorities — the same institutions that set the standards healthcare systems around the world are built on. No guesswork. No wellness trends. Just published, peer-reviewed science applied to your results.

🇬🇧

NHS

The UK's National Health Service — a government-funded authority with statutory obligations for health standards, serving over 67 million people.

🌍

WHO

The World Health Organization — the United Nations' directing and coordinating authority on international health, with 194 member countries.

🔎

NICE

The UK's National Institute for Health and Care Excellence — an independent statutory body whose technology appraisals are legally binding within the NHS.

🇨🇦

Medical Council of Canada

Canada's federal regulatory authority for medical qualification standards, established in 1912 — the national gatekeeper of Canadian healthcare.

Every range is traceable to a published, peer-reviewed international standard. If it isn't cited, it isn't in the app.

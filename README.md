# OptimizeHer
Women are routinely told their lab results are 'normal' while continuing to experience real symptoms. OptimizeHer gives them a fuller picture — comparing lab values against optimal ranges sourced from internationally recognized health authorities.

It currently covers the five markers most commonly overlooked in women's healthcare: TSH, Ferritin, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, and HDL Cholesterol. Additional markers to be added soon. 

All analysis happens on your device. No data collected, stored, or transmitted.

Sources
Every optimal range in OptimizeHer is traceable to a published, peer-reviewed international standard:

🇬🇧 NHS — UK National Health Service
🌍 WHO — World Health Organization
🔎 NICE — National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (UK)
🇨🇦 MCC — Medical Council of Canada

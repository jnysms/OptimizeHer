# OptimizeHer
Women are routinely told their lab results are 'normal' while continuing to experience real symptoms. OptimizeHer gives them a fuller picture — comparing lab values against optimal ranges sourced from peer-reviewed research.

It currently covers four markers most commonly overlooked in women's healthcare: TSH, Ferritin, Vitamin B12, and HDL Cholesterol. Additional markers to be added soon.

All analysis happens on your device. No data collected, stored, or transmitted.

## Sources
Every optimal range in OptimizeHer is traceable to a published, peer-reviewed source:

- **The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology** — TSH optimal range
- **Canadian Medical Association Journal** — Ferritin/iron deficiency in women
- **Annals of Neurology** — Vitamin B12 and neurological outcomes
- **European Heart Journal** — HDL cholesterol ranges for women
